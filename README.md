# Selected Work

### 🤖 AI Lead Management & CRM Automation

**AI-powered lead capture, scoring and automated follow-up built around Odoo CRM.**

The platform brings leads from multiple channels into one pipeline, uses AI to prioritize opportunities, automates follow-ups and gives sales teams a clearer view of pipeline performance.

**Stack**

`Python` `FastAPI` `OpenAI API` `Odoo CRM` `React` `PostgreSQL`

→ [View case study](https://www.zavior.org/portfolio/ai-lead-management-crm-automation-platform)

---

### 📦 Custom ERP & Inventory Management

**Real-time ERP and warehouse management for a Dubai trading & distribution company.**

The solution connected purchasing, suppliers, warehouses and inventory into a centralized Odoo environment with real-time stock visibility and automated reorder management.

**Stack**

`Odoo` `Python` `PostgreSQL` `Barcode` `Power BI`

→ [View case study](https://www.zavior.org/portfolio/custom-erp-inventory-management-dubai-trading)

---

### 🏭 Manufacturing ERP & CRM

**Connected sales, production and inventory into one operational platform.**

**Impact**

**Stack**
